DAILY SUNWATCH

Sunny is watching the Sun. Automated solar and space-weather observations, updates, and original content from the Sun to near-Earth space.

About

Solar observation, with a little personality.

Daily Sunwatch is an automated solar and space-weather project focused on activity across the Sun and near-Earth environment. It combines regularly updated observations with original visual content designed to make space weather informative, approachable, and fun.

What We Watch

From solar activity to conditions near Earth.

Daily Sunwatch follows several parts of the Sun–Earth system and turns public space-weather data into clear, regularly updated observations.

☀ Solar Activity & Flares

Solar X-ray activity, flare events, and active regions across the Sun.

◉ Coronal Mass Ejections

Coronal activity and CME events reported through public space-weather sources.

↝ Solar Wind & Geomagnetic Activity

Solar-wind speed, IMF Bz, Kp, and other conditions in the near-Earth environment.

⌁ Aurora Conditions

Geomagnetic conditions and ingredients that can support auroral activity.
