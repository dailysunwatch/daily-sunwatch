Daily Sunwatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Daily Sunwatch is an automated application used to publish space weather updates to the Daily Sunwatch Facebook Page.

Daily Sunwatch does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from Facebook users.

The application uses Meta's services only as necessary to publish and manage content on the Daily Sunwatch Facebook Page.

Daily Sunwatch retrieves solar and space weather information from publicly available NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center data sources.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: dailysunwatch@gmail.com
